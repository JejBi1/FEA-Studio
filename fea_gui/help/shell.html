Shell model

Thin-walled parts (plates, bent sheet metal, surface parts from STEP) as shells: a mid-surface with thickness (S6 elements in CalculiX). Far fewer elements than 3D; gives top / bottom, membrane and bending stress. Set up in Solve, group Shell model.

Procedure

  1. Supports and loads as in 3D on part faces. Surface parts also take edges (Edge filter E); Fixed support from selection uses selected edges when no faces are selected.
  2. Check analyze the shell model instead of 3D. The table shows detected type and thickness; an unrecognized part is red, with the reason.
  3. Surface part from STEP: select the row, enter Part thickness, press Set thickness (plates from solids take it from geometry; an entered value overrides).
  4. Run; in Results select case Shells: … and the quantity.

Detection of plates and sheet metal

  • Plate: two parallel planar faces of opposite normals (largest area) at distance t, constant thickness (volume = t·(A₁ + A₂)/2 within 2 %). Slenderness √A / t ≥ 5; below 10 a warning (thick plate).
  • Sheet metal with cylindrical bends: mid-surface by offset. Plates joined in T or L: mid-surface extended by t/2.

Results

Contours on the mid-surface (shape per the Deformation option). Quantities: von Mises max of surfaces, top, middle, bottom; membrane (mid-surface) and bending ((top − bottom)/2) stress; principal stresses; σx, σy of top and bottom; displacements; thickness. The probe shows the displayed layer. The summary lists reactions (forces, moments about the reference point) and the equilibrium check.

Limitations

  • Supports: fixed and prescribed displacement. Loads: force, pressure, gravity, rotation (no local coordinate systems).
  • No bolts, welds, heat, 3D printing, orthotropy, contacts or dynamics; no mixing with solids.
  • Verified with Sh1–Sh7 (cantilever against 3D 0.06%, T&W-K plate 1%, Lamé pipe, plate with a hole, T-beam, bent sheet): python -m verification.run_all Sh1 … Sh7.
