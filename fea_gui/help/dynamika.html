Dynamics

Modes, harmonic, transient, random vibration (ASD) and shock (response spectrum): CalculiX modes + modal superposition. Added mass is entered here.

Damping is uncertain (±50 %); double ζ halves the resonance peak. Measure it (hammer test, decay) with From measurement…; indicative ζ: Friction and damping.

Procedure

  1. Check compute dynamics; modes cover 1.5–2× the highest excitation frequency, effective mass in the excited direction ≥ 90 % (checked).
  2. Damping: modal ζ (default 2 %), Rayleigh (ζ at two frequencies), structural η (harmonic only, η = 2ζ), or per material.
  3. Switch on a response, set the excitation: force = load case × history, or base motion (g / mm/s², or mm) along an axis (all supports move with the base).
  4. Add probes (point or faces) for FRF, time history, PSD.
  5. Run in Solve: “Dynamics: …” items, Dynamics chart tab.

Test profiles (structure only)

Test profile menu: IEC 60068-2-6 (sine), -2-64 (random), -2-27 (shock), -2-47 (mounting). No levels, ranges or durations are built in – take them from the standard. The “IEC 60068-2-6: const. displacement / acceleration” generator takes x̂ [mm] and a [g], gives f_c = √(a/x̂)/2π and the acceleration table.

Added mass

  • Point mass [kg] at the centre of gravity: rigid coupling (also Ixx, Iyy, Izz [kg·m²]; stiffens the face) or distributing (translational only).
  • Surface mass [kg/m²] over a face (PCB, coating).
  • Acts in gravity, rotation, frequencies, dynamics.

Results

  • Harmonic: frequency slider, phase φ, “absolute” = displacement + base motion; “Amplitude over phase” ignores φ; envelope = maximum over frequencies and where.
  • Transient, Random (RMS, 3σ, damage, life), Shock, mode shapes.
  • Dynamics tab: FRF, time history, response PSD, input ASD, pulse SRS, reactions; mode table (frequency, damping, effective mass; click = shape in 3D).

Not combinable with contact (linear model needed), the 2D section or shells.
