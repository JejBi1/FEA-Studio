Fatigue

Computes fatigue damage and life from the static results: stress in time is the sum of load cases multiplied by the load history, cycles are counted by rainflow and damage is accumulated by Palmgren–Miner with the material's S-N curve.

Principle

Each load case is a unit case. The load history (one block) gives the multiple by which each case acts in time, and the stress in a node is the superposition:

σ(t) = Σ fk(t) · σk   (linear elasticity; several channels on one case are added)

Example: case “Force 200 N” with a sine channel, mean 0.5, amplitude 0.5 = pulsating 0–200 N. The block repeats Design number of blocks times over the life.

  1. Compute statics (Solve).
  2. Enter the load history channels and the design number of blocks.
  3. Choose method, mean stress correction, surface and S-N curve.
  4. Click Run fatigue analysis.

CSV import

  • Two columns time; multiple, or one column of multiples (time = row number). Header rows are skipped.
  • The separator ; or tab takes precedence, so the comma is decimal (0;1,5). A comma separator applies only with a decimal point in the file or a text header (0,1.5).
  • Commas only and no points (1,5) is ambiguous and rejected – use a semicolon or a decimal point.

Tip: a mean stress from permanent loads (bolt preload, self-weight, thermal stress) is a channel with a constant multiple of 1: Points (0; 1) and (1; 1).

S-N estimate from Rm (without tests)

  • Steel (E ≥ 150 GPa): Se' = 0.5·Rm at 10⁶ cycles (at most 700 MPa), 0.9·Rm at 10³, endurance limit below 10⁶ (Shigley).
  • Aluminium (E 40–150 GPa): 0.4·Rm (at most 130 MPa) at 5·10⁸, no endurance limit (Haibach below the knee).
  • Plastic: 0.25·Rm at 10⁶, k = 10 – indicative only; calibrate from tests.

Marin factors reduce the knee amplitude; in the Rm estimate the point 0.9·Rm at 10³ cycles remains. Both apply to a smooth specimen – the notch effect is in the FE stress (fine mesh at the notch).

ε-N method (strain-life)

  • The elastic FE stress history (signed von Mises or max. principal stress; no critical plane) is converted by the notch rule to the local elastic-plastic strain on the cyclic Ramberg-Osgood curve; rainflow, the Coffin-Manson-Basquin curve and Palmgren–Miner give the damage.
  • The parameters σ'f, b, ε'f, c, K', n' are estimated from Rm and E (Bäumel-Seeger uniform material law) – indicative, verify by tests. The “ε-N used” line shows the values. Measured parameters can be given only through the Python API or the study file (FatigueSettings.en_params, see Automation); the GUI and the MCP server have no input for them.
  • Surface, reliability, size and other Marin factors and the S-N curve are not used (greyed out). Not available for 3D-printed parts.

PragTic database

The S-N from PragTic database dialog holds 647 literature records. Type any grade (12050, C45, 1.0503, AISI 1045, S45C…); equivalents are found through the grade converter. The Match column shows match, ≈ match (approximate equivalent) or similar (same family, similar composition). Only with S-N data (tension-compression / bending) hides records without fatigue data. The right side shows the full record with sources; choose an S-N variant (tension-compression limit, Basquin, bending limit) in S-N curve for analysis. also take over Rm and Re into the material overwrites the study material's Rm and Re (the mean stress correction uses Rm). Use S-N or double-click takes the curve over.

Data source and citation. The data belong to the author of the database: Papuga, J.: PragTic – material database of fatigue properties (Fatigue Data Repository). Czech Technical University in Prague, Faculty of Mechanical Engineering. https://www.pragtic.com/vmat.php. For each curve taken over, FEA Studio states the record number and the original source; the Word report adds the citation. Cite the database and the original source in your own documentation too.

The database is not distributed with the program (the author has not granted redistribution). For your own use download it from pragtic.com with python -m fea_core.fatigue.pragtic_fetch; fatigue works without it (estimate from Rm or a manual S-N).

Analysis and results

  • Run fatigue analysis runs in the background. With proportional channels rainflow is computed once – under 1 s. The critical plane takes tens of seconds; with non-proportional loading and thousands of time points, minutes.
  • The first run after start may be slower (kernel compilation).
  • The fatigue result is not saved in the project (the settings are); any change of settings or study discards it.

How to read the result

  • The result is the local stress of a smooth specimen. Do not judge worst locations in singularities (sharp internal corners, support edges) by the nodal value – refine, add fillets or use a notch method (see Results).
  • Signed von Mises has an ambiguous sign for pure shear (σ1 = −σ3): use the critical plane for torsion and non-proportional loading.
  • A nonzero prescribed displacement sits in every case and multiplies in superposition. Put it in a separate case and compute the others with zero displacement.
  • Welds and bolts are assessed separately (FAT class, VDI 2230 σ_ASV) – Joints.
  • Printed parts use the Hill equivalent in print axes (shell and core) with print S-N (indicative, after Ezeh & Susmel 2019; no Marin factors). The S-N box shows the print S-N directly (SD in XY and SD_z normal to layers); a custom S-N applies to XY, and without a k2 it continues with the print slope (plastics have no endurance limit). See 3D printing.
  • Bearing load is not linear in force direction (pressure on the half of the hole facing the force): superposing cases with a bearing load gives a different stress than reality for a rotating force (warning).

Fatigue with contacts, from a mechanism and from dynamics

  • Contacts (nonlinear): superposition does not apply. Fatigue is possible only from a step sequence (Contacts → Step sequence, cycles ≥ 2). The history is the ends of the steps of the last cycle (the first cycle with slip may differ); load channels are not used. The program checks that the response has stabilized between the last two cycles (change up to 0.1 %), otherwise add cycles. The course within a step is not considered. Branches (“cases separately”) end with an error.
  • Mechanism: transferring the history from Motion creates unit cases (joint reactions, inertia) and history channels; fatigue then superposes as usual.
  • Dynamics: fatigue from a sine sweep and spectral fatigue of random vibration (Dirlik, Tovo–Benasciutti, narrow-band) are set and computed in Dynamics; results appear as “Dynamics: …” items in Results. They need Rm (estimated S-N) or an entered S-N.

In the examples

Example 1 – printed bracket

  • One channel: Eye load 200 N: sine, mean 0.5, amplitude 0.5, 1 cycles, phase 0° → pulsating 0–200 N, design number of blocks 1e5 (100 000 cycles).
  • Proportional loading → instant analysis. The whole part is printed, so the Hill equivalent with print S-N applies – indicative; without test calibration not proof.
  • Try: a Self-weight channel with constant multiple 1 (mean stress), or 1e6 blocks, and watch the safety drop.

Example 2 – assembly

  • Two channels: Force on the arm eye sine 0–1 (pulsating 0–300 N) and Lateral force on the column sine ±1 (±250 N) at 90° phase; design number of blocks 2e5.
  • Non-proportional → non-proportional loading. Compare signed von Mises and the critical plane (Findley) – for the S235 column and base (S-N estimated from Rm) they may differ; the purple segment shows the critical plane at the worst location.
  • Bolt preload and thermal stress are not in the history; add channels “Bolt preload” and “Heating in operation” with constant multiple 1 to include them as mean stress.
  • Weld roots and toes are singularities on the map; evaluate welds and bolts in the Joints – utilization dock.

Common problems

Message / symptomCause and remedy
“Compute statics first (Solve step) – the results have no mechanical load case.”Fatigue needs static results; a heat-only study has no fatigue.
“Fatigue with contact / nonlinear analysis in branches is not available …”A contact study computed case by case has no history. In Contacts define a step sequence with ≥ 2 cycles and recompute.
“Load case '…' is not in the results”The channel refers to a renamed or deleted case. Delete and re-add the channel, or recompute statics.
“CSV cannot be loaded: CSV is ambiguous (a comma may be a separator or a decimal comma…)”Save the CSV with separator ; (Excel in Czech does this itself) or with a decimal point.
“Rm of the material is needed for the S-N estimate”Add Rm in Materials or enter a custom S-N.
“The ratio f₋₁/t₋₁ must be in the range (1; 2⟩…”Findley does not suit brittle materials – choose the normal stress criterion.
“Critical plane with non-proportional loading and N time points is slow…”Shorten the block: fewer cycles of the same shape, more repetitions (design number of blocks).
