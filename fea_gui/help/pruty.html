Members (frames, trusses, beams)

The Beams step defines a frame: nodes, members (straight, arched, truss), cross-sections (EN library, parametric, custom from a CAD face), supports and springs, connection of a member to a solid face, and EN 1993-1-1 check inputs. A frame can stand alone (a study without CAD) or be part of a model with solids.

Procedure

  1. Study without CAD: Model step → New frame (no CAD) (also in Tools). With CAD go to the Members step.
  2. Cross-sections tab: library profile (series, or search “IPE 200”, “HEB200”, “L50x5”, “CHS 60.3x4”), parametric shape or planar CAD face → Add cross-section.
  3. Nodes tab: name and coordinates → Add node.
  4. Members tab: start and end node, cross-section, material, kind, orientation, hinges → Add member.
  5. Support tab: node and restrained components (fixed, hinge) or springs.
  6. Member loads in Loads (node force / moment, distributed member load, gravity). Material in Materials; without CAD, Assign to all sets the default.

Tip: a click on a node or member in 3D selects its table row (on Support / Connection it inserts the node into the form; in Loads it selects the target of the member load).

Member axes and signs

Local x runs from start to end node. z = “up” of the section (web of an I profile). y = z × x (strong axis of an I profile: bending by My, deflection in z). Automatic: z = projection of global Z (vertical member: global X). N > 0 = tension.

Analysis and results

  • Frame alone: built-in solver, exact Timoshenko element (double precision, one element per member; diagrams N, V, T, M and displacements integrated analytically), with node and reaction equilibrium check. Members with a solid run in CalculiX (element U1); the built-in solver can be switched to CalculiX U1 for comparison.
  • Natural frequencies (Solve step; consistent mass; truss members also vibrate locally) and linear buckling of a frame alone: built-in solver (geometric stiffness from axial forces, Engesser shear correction – α_cr per EN 1993-1-1, 5.2.1). Buckling of members with solids is not available yet.
  • Results: Members – σ_eq, displacement, N, Vy, Vz, T, My, Mz, EN 1993 utilization (diagrams perpendicular to the member), Members tab (extremes and check per member), mode and buckling shapes, reactions.
  • EN 1993-1-1 check: section class, resistance (6.2), buckling, lateral-torsional buckling and interaction (6.3, Annex B) in all load cases. Arches with R/h < 10: Winkler–Bach shown for information.
  • Member temperature (uniform ΔT, gradient across the section): built-in solver, entered via MCP (fea_frame_load_temperature); restrained expansion N = −E·A·α·ΔT.

Beam calculator

Tools → Beam calculator…: closed-form formulas without FEA – simply supported / fixed / cantilever / continuous beam (V, M, w, reactions, extremes, stress M/W), curved member (Winkler–Bach), Euler buckling, EN 1993-1-1 member check with entered forces. Load and deflection positive downward; M positive = tension at the bottom.

Common problems

  • “Member … has no material” – set the study default (Materials → Assign to all) or the member's material.
  • “The frame structure has no supports” – add a support or a connection to a body.
  • Mechanism (singular) – hinges at both ends in torsion, or a node whose members are all hinged with no rotation support.
  • Buckling without result – no compression in the reference state. Linear buckling uses only axial force (no warping or lateral-torsional buckling); assess stability by EN 1993-1-1.
