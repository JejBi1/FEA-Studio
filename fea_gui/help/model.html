1. Model

Loads a STEP file or a .fea project, checks the geometry and lists the parts. Later steps refer to parts by name and faces by shape and position.

Preparing the model in CAD: Export from CAD (STEP settings, Send to FEA Studio for Fusion, SOLIDWORKS and Inventor, materials from CAD).

A new study from STEP gets:

  • material Steel S235 for all parts (change in Materials); CAD metadata (<same name>.json) next to the STEP give parts the CAD material if it is in the library (Export from CAD);
  • element size ≈ model diagonal / 30, rounded down to 1 / 2 / 2.5 / 5 × 10ⁿ mm (240 mm model → 5 mm);
  • an empty Load case 1.

Frame structure without CAD

New frame (no CAD) (also in Tools) creates a study from members only (frame, truss, beam). 3D printing, Joints, Supports, Heat and Mesh are disabled; define the structure in Members. A .fea without STEP opens like any other (example 6).

What a STEP file should contain

  • Solid bodies: faces and shells without volume are ignored.
  • Assembly with part names: name from the instance or component (Fusion: “Deska:1”; several bodies “Deska:1_1”, “Deska:1_2”). Generic names (“SOLID”, “Body1”…) are replaced by the component name; duplicates get “ (2)”, “ (3)”.
  • Units: converted to mm.
  • Bolts, nuts, washers with a standard name (“ISO 4017 - M8 x 30 - 8.8”) become Joints. Welds as separate bodies (name “Svar…”, colour or layer).
  • Hidden bodies are not exported from Fusion.

Parts in the study tree

The Parts branch lists all parts; Info shows material or status:

  • bolt (beam) – bolt, nut or washer: not meshed, a beam replaces it (Joints);
  • excluded – excluded from the analysis;
  • no material (red).

Assemblies: touching parts are bonded (without a contact pair)

The mesh is continuous: parts touching by a face share nodes and are rigidly connected (no friction, no gap). The table Connected parts (shared mesh) in Joints lists them (Part A / Part B / Contact [mm²]). Parts touching only by an edge or point are not reliably connected – check them.

For separation, friction, press fit or compression only, define a pair in Contacts (Find touching parts scans the assembly); pair parts are meshed separately and the analysis is nonlinear. Bolted-joint interfaces: check box bolted joint interfaces as contact (Contacts).

Switching bodies on and off: Show / Analyze

  • Show – visible in 3D. Display only (analysis, mesh, results unchanged); a hidden body cannot be picked (to see inner parts). Saved in the project; Ctrl+Z undoes it.
  • Analyze – in the analysis. A switched-off body (magnet, pin, cable, bought part) is not meshed or analyzed and is transparent in 3D. Discards the mesh; selections, supports and loads of other bodies remain.

A tick in a selected row applies to all selected rows. Right-click the table: hide / show, Show only the selected bodies, Show all, Include all in the analysis, By name (e.g. bolt, M6*, *magnet*; without an asterisk part of the name suffices). The buttons below the table and the right-click menu of a part in the tree do the same.

Bodies hidden in CAD (Fusion assembly from .f3d / .f3z, metadata "hidden": true) load hidden and excluded. Parts can also be excluded in Joints (group Parts excluded from the analysis).

Face selection and selectors

Supports, loads, thermal conditions and refinement use face selections. A selection stores a selector (part name, face type, area, centroid, a point with normal, envelope), not a face number, so a project survives a new export of the same model.

  • Names follow purpose and sequence: “Fixed support 1”, “Displacement 2”, “Cylindrical support 1”, “Force 3”, “Pressure 1”, “Bearing load 1”, “Refinement 1”… Results reactions use them.
  • A face is found only on a nearly exact match (area, centroid, envelope within 0.01 % of the face size); a changed face (size, position, split) is not found.
  • Replace faces of an existing support or load with Faces from selection in its step.

Bolts, washers and nuts show “bolt (beam)” and are not meshed. The arm sits on the base plate by its flange and is bonded in the mesh (Connected parts table); welds are separate bodies.

Common problems

  • “The file cannot be opened: Cannot read STEP …” – invalid or damaged STEP; export again (AP214 / AP242).
  • “Some selected faces were not found in the model (did the model change?)” – faces changed (size, split, renamed part). Select again and press Faces from selection on the item. During the analysis it reads “Some selected faces, edges, or points were not found”.
  • A renamed part got a different material – materials follow part names; a new name gets the default. Check the Material column, reassign in Materials.
  • “The volume mesh was not generated – the geometry probably self-intersects or parts touch tangentially (cylinder in a slot, coincident faces).” – parts overlap or touch along a line (pin in a slot). Remove the overlap in CAD or exclude the part.
  • “Some parts are not connected to a support (free bodies – the problem would be singular)” – the part touches nothing by a face and is neither bolted nor welded. The program offers to exclude it; otherwise fix the geometry or add a joint.
  • “Selection '…': part '…' is excluded (not meshed)” – a support or load lies on an excluded part (or bolt). Move it to a load-carrying part or include the part.
