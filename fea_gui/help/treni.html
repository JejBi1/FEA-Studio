Friction and damping (values with sources)

The contact friction coefficient μ is always an estimate. The library holds material pairs with sources and picks μ by purpose: μ_min for force transfer by friction, μ_max for mechanism resistance. Where it matters, measure μ (inclined plane: μs = tan α) and save it to the user library (Tools → Calculators → Friction library).

Selection rules

  • Force transfer by friction (bolted joint F_Q, press fit, brake, snap fit against pull-out) → μ_min.
  • Drive force, assembly force, guide jamming, tightening torque → μ_max and μs; rolling bearings at start-up 2×.
  • Self-locking that must hold (screw, wedge) → check with μ_min.
  • Compute both bounds as variants.
  • Plastics: μ falls with load (PLA μs 0.30 → 0.20 at 30 → 70 N), rises sharply near Tg; the PV limit applies.

CalculiX contact supports only Coulomb friction (μ and stick slope λ); velocity-dependent friction (Stribeck, LuGre) belongs to mechanism kinematics.
