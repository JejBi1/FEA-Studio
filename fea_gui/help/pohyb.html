Motion (mechanisms)

Rigid-body mechanisms: animation, kinematics, joint reactions, driver loads, energies. An instant or the whole history goes to FEA as load cases. Exudyn runs in a separate process.

Procedure

  1. Bodies: parts moving together (“Body for each part” creates all). Mark one the ground (may have no parts). “Compute masses” takes mass and centre of gravity from CAD (printed parts indicative); a body without parts needs them entered.
  2. Joints: “Suggest from geometry” finds pins in coaxial holes; confirm in the dialog. Others: type, bodies A → B, origin and axis (“Axis from selection”), stops with restitution, friction. “From Fusion metadata…” loads Fusion joints.
  3. Drivers and forces: kinematic (angle, displacement, velocity) or force (torque, force) driver; law constant, table, sine, polynomial or S-curve (limited jerk); “Preview” shows both derivatives. Also spring, damper, torsion and gas spring, bushing, external loads, gravity.
  4. Analysis: type, duration, outputs, collision check. DOF check = Grübler formula, mobility, redundant constraints. Run motion.
  5. Results: summary, animation (“CAD position” returns the parts), chart of any quantity, “Find maximum”, “Transfer to FEA…”.

Redundant constraints (planar mechanism of revolute joints in 3D, lid on two coaxial hinges): motion is right, but reactions split between joints ambiguously (sum holds). Use revolute + ball / cylindrical, a compliant bushing, or make redundant joints supports in the transfer.

Joint friction

From the joint reaction: Coulomb μ·|R|·r (pin: clearance, worn-in, fitted), viscous c·v, Stribeck, rolling bearing M = μ·F·d/2, lead screw. Coefficients: Friction and damping.

Transfer to FEA

  • Instant (given times) or instant of the maximum of a quantity → load case: reactions as remote loads (RBE3) on joint faces, inertia and gravity as the “Inertia” load in body axes.
  • One joint is the support (remote support, inertia relief); its reaction must match MBD – Support check compares.
  • Whole history → unit load cases (6 components per joint, 12 inertial) and fatigue channels (Fatigue step).
  • A joint needs a face (hole, pin): automatic for suggested joints, otherwise “Face A / B from selection”.

Solvers and flexible bodies

Exudyn (default); Chrono (contact of CAD bodies) is optional, greyed out without it. A flexible body (Craig–Bampton, fixed-interface modes): dynamic analysis, isotropic materials only.

Collision check without contact: bodies pass through each other; only minimum distance and first collision are reported.
