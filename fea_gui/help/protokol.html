Word report

The Report… button (Ctrl+P) creates a technical report in Word (.docx): setup, mesh, results with figures, joint checks, fatigue, 3D printing, assumptions and warnings, with a pass / fail rating.

Procedure

  1. Calculate statics (and heat). For fatigue in the report, calculate it first in Fatigue – the report uses the GUI result.
  2. Click Report… in the top bar or press Ctrl+P.
  3. Fill in the details, check the path, click Generate report.
  4. It runs in the background (“Generating report…” in the status bar) and opens in Word when finished.

Without results the report holds only the setup (“Results are not available – the report will contain only the setup.”). A heat-only study gives a full thermal report.

Word report dialog

The top line says what the report will contain, e.g. “Load cases: 6 · thermal cases: 2 · joint checks: will be run · fatigue: no (Fatigue step)”.

Report contents

  • Title block – study, geometry, company, author, date, program; subject.
  • Summary – statics table by case: max. displacement, max. σ_vM, σ_vM away from singularities, min. safety factor to Re, imbalance, rating (k ≥ required). Thermal cases; joints (max. utilization), fatigue (max. D, min. safety factor), 3D printing (min. Tsai-Wu/Hill safety factor, without creep).
  • 1 Analysis setup – parts (volume, mass from the mesh), materials with sources (printing: solid extrusion properties), supports with face areas, load cases and loads, bolts (designation, grade, grip length, preload), welds (a, length), thermal cases; a model figure per case.
  • 2 Mesh and solver – element type, size, refinement, nodes, elements, DOF, min. scaled Jacobian with quality rating, solver.
  • 3 Results – per thermal and load case: values, maxima locations with singularity classification, reactions (forces, moments), equilibrium check, safety factor to Re by part, maps of von Mises, displacement, safety factor.
  • Bolted and welded joints – utilization overview, optional detailed checks (VDI 2230, EN 1993-1-8, EN 1993-1-9 / IIW).
  • Fatigue – method (as in the Fatigue step), S-N curves with sources, worst locations, maps.
  • 3D printing – settings, safety factor by part, creep; always a red warning about plastic creep.
  • Contacts (if defined) – pairs with type, μ with source and purpose, K, λ, interference, formulation, step sequence; results per step, pressure and status maps, force–travel curves; always red warnings about nonlinear analysis, μ estimate, K/λ penalty and possible partial result.
  • Plasticity (if a material has plasticity) – material curves (table and plot of the *PLASTIC points), max. PEEQ per step and where, EN 1993-1-5 Annex C and ASME VIII-2 5.3.3.1 utilization with Table 5.7 constants, residual stress of unloading steps, PEEQ map; always a red warning about the assumptions.
  • Dynamics (if enabled) – modes with effective mass, damping, excitation and test profile, FRF, envelopes, RMS and 3σ, spectral fatigue, shock and SRS, added mass; always a red warning about damping uncertainty (±50 %).
  • Motion (mechanism) (if computed) – bodies, joints, drivers, largest joint reactions and their instant, driver forces and power, reaction plots, equilibrium and power balance, load cases transferred to FEA; always a red warning about rigid bodies, friction estimate and redundant constraints.
  • Assumptions, limitations and warnings – linear elasticity, parts joined by a shared mesh (no contact pair), contact and dynamics assumptions where used, all analysis warnings.
  • Conclusion – text of the Conclusion field.

Numbers follow the report language (decimal separator, ·10ⁿ, minus “−”). Reactions below 10⁻⁵ of the total load show as 0 (noise). The safety factor map is omitted if it is ≥ 10 everywhere.

Note: the report is a supporting document, not an automatic assessment. “PASS” rests on the maximum at a node and cannot tell if it is the governing location. Assess singularities yourself and state it in the Conclusion.

Without the GUI (command line)

report generates a report from a study (study.json) or a .fea project. A project with results is not recalculated; a study without results is calculated. Fatigue is calculated if the study has a load history; joints are checked.

In the examples

Example 1 – printed bracket

  • Summary: three cases (eye load 200 N, self-weight, creep case) and a 3D printing row with the min. safety factor. For PLA the safety factor to Re is indicative; the print safety factor governs.
  • 3D printing chapter: 3 perimeters, gyroid 20 %, Tsai-Wu, creep 1000 h / 30 °C, red creep warning.
  • The Fatigue chapter appears only if fatigue was calculated before generating.

Example 2 – assembly

  • Setup adds tables of bolts (2× M6 8.8, preload) and welds (a = 4 mm), and a Thermal cases chapter with boundary condition figures.
  • Results start with the thermal cases (balance, temperature map), then six mechanical cases including Bolt preload and the creep case.
  • Bolted and welded joints fills in automatically; with detailed checks the report is much longer.

Common problems

Message / symptomCause and solution
“The report could not be generated: PermissionError …”A file of the same name is open in Word – close it or rename.
“Open a model or project first.”The report needs an open study.
Fatigue is missing in the reportAny change of fatigue settings or the study discards the GUI fatigue result. Recalculate; the dialog must show “fatigue: yes”.
“Report – warning: Figure … : …” in the logA figure could not be rendered; the report is made without it. Retry, or without images.
FAIL rating because of a singularityThe summary also gives σ_vM away from singularities. Assess the location (Results) and justify it in the Conclusion.
The report did not openWord or another .docx program is missing. The file exists – open it elsewhere (LibreOffice, Word Online).
